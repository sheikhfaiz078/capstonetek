The industry challenge

Once a product leaves the shelf, most brands lose sight of it

Product-driven brands face three critical hurdles after their products leave the shelf.

Counterfeit products

Duplication damages brand equity, erodes consumer trust, and directly impacts revenue.

Information gaps

Physical packaging limits the educational material and instructions a brand can share with the buyer.

Consumer disconnect

Once a product is purchased, brands often lose the ability to build a direct, ongoing relationship with the end user.
